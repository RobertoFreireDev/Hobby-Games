GAME SUMMARIES
==============


THE EMBER SEAL  (games/emberseal)

Play The Ember Seal to carry a burnt coin across a dying valley and find out
which of the endings you get. Every choice sticks: what you pack, who you
trust, and whether you ever put the seal on.

Arrows to pick, X to choose.

Game by Roberto Freire


GRIDLOCK  (games/gridlock)

Play Gridlock to slide the red block out of the jam before the board
detonates. You get a fixed number of moves per level - one wasted move and
the whole thing blows up.

Arrows to move, X to grab and drop a block, hold O to reset.

Game by Roberto Freire


KANGUROO  (games/kanguroo)

Play Kanguroo to hop through the desert and land every crate on an orange
pad. Roo hops two tiles at a time, one if something is in the way, and water
sinks roo and crates alike.

Arrows to hop, X to undo, hold O to retry the level.

Game by Roberto Freire


LOCKSTEP  (games/lockstep)

Play Lockstep to move everybody at once - one press and every piece on the
board steps together. Get them all home inside par to unlock the next level.

Arrows to step, X to play, O to go back.

Game by Roberto Freire


MONSTERGRAM  (games/monstergram)

Play Monstergram to solve a nonogram that fights back. The numbers count runs
of monsters, every blank tile is safe to dig, and you swing a sword at
anything you uncover wrong.

X to dig or fight, O to flag a tile.

Game by Roberto Freire


MOONFALL  (games/moonfall)

Play Moonfall to find the wolf hiding in a village of eight. Every night you
walk around, ask people what they heard, and write it in your notebook -
one of them is lying, and you have to name them before the moon comes up.

Arrows to walk and choose, O to talk and accuse, X for the notebook.

Game by Roberto Freire


PATTERN HUNT  (games/patternhunt)

Play Pattern Hunt to find sets of three in a grid of shapes. A chain counts
when its three digits are all the same, a run in any order, or all one
colour - diagonals count too.

Arrows to move, X to pick cells, O for how to play.

Game by Roberto Freire


PEG DUNGEON  (games/pegsolitairerpg)

Play Peg Dungeon to clear a floor of monsters that never move. It is peg
solitaire with a hero in it: jump stones over monsters to kill them, jump a
sword to block damage, and route every jump so you are the one left standing.

Arrows to jump, O to select a stone/item, X to drink a potion.

Game by Roberto Freire


PORTAL FLOW  (games/portalflow)

Play Portal Flow to join every pair of dots and fill every last tile - a
connected board with one empty square is not solved. Some boards carry a
portal pair: a pipe that enters one comes out of the other, and it can only
ever belong to one colour.

Arrows to draw, X to grab a dot, O to undo or hold to clear.

Game by Roberto Freire


QUADRIS  (games/quadris)

Play Quadris to survive tetris from all four sides at once. Blocks fall in
from every edge toward the middle - clear a full ring to score.

Arrows to move and rotate, X to start.

Game by Roberto Freire


RING SORT  (games/ringsort)

Play Ring Sort to sort eight stacks of little creatures until every pile is a
single colour. Some colours refuse to sit on each other - the plates along the
bottom name the pairs, and the two of them scowl at you if you try it anyway.

Arrows to move, X to lift a creature and X again to drop it.

Game by Roberto Freire


ROOMMATES  (games/roommates)

Play Roommates to seat a house full of difficult people and keep everyone
happy. Drifters, homebodies, loners and clingers all want different
neighbours, and a red outline means somebody is mad.

Arrows to move people, X to place, O to go back.

Game by Roberto Freire


ROTAMAZE  (games/rotamaze)

Play Rotamaze to escape a maze that turns. You cannot go around the walls -
you spin them out of the way, one tile at a time, and walk through the gap
you just made.

Arrows to walk (bump a wall to aim at it), X to turn the faced tile,
O to rewind, hold O to restart.

Game by Roberto Freire


SNAKEBOX  (games/snakebox)

Play Snakebox to fill every tile in the box without biting yourself or
boxing yourself in. No food, no growth timer - just you, the walls, and how
much of the room you can cover before you run out of room.

Arrows to turn, X to start.

Game by Roberto Freire


STACK RUNNER  (games/stackrunner)

Play Stack Runner to get down sixteen floors on a stack of cards. Every step
you take spends a card you found, so what you pick up decides how far you can
still go.

Arrows to spend a card and move, X to use an item, O to undo.

Game by Roberto Freire


STONE LOGIC  (games/stone)

Play Stone Logic to clear a pile of stones by deduction instead of luck. Take
any stone nothing sits on and match two of a face - covered stones hide their
faces, so you read the board's laws and the tally to work out what is under
there.

Arrows to pick a stone, X to take, O for the laws, hold O to retry.

Game by Roberto Freire


TRIAD  (games/triad)

Play Triad to spot trios in a grid of cards. Three cards make a trio when
every property - shape, colour, number, fill - is either all the same or all
different across them. One bad property fails the whole thing.

Arrows to move, X to pick a card, O for the tutorial.

Game by Roberto Freire


HIGH ROLLER  (games/yahtzee)

Play High Roller to beat the house over thirteen rounds of dice. Roll, hold
what you want, and score it in the box that gets you the most - fill the top
half for the +35 bonus.

X to roll and score, O to hold dice, up/down to pick a box.

Game by Roberto Freire
